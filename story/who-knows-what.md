  copied the page onto the PC.

- **S6 — Frank saw the 4x4** that night and has stayed silent (drunk, scared
  of his record, sure no one believes him).
- **S7 — Angel's real evening** (wine coolers at the Gene's back lot; her
  first statement was a lie to protect her cousin).
- **S8 — Chad's alibi hole** — during the meeting window he was writing an
  apology email and drinking behind Gene's [PROPOSED: also buying pills for a
  teammate's injury — which is why he won't say where he was; ironic S1 tie].
- **S9 — The code word ("junebug")** — Casey & Sadie's private "come get me"
  token [CANONIZED in-game]. Two people on Earth were TOLD it — but Casey set
  it as her screen saver text, floating on her idle monitor every night. The
  list of people who could have SEEN it is exactly: the household. This is a
  deliberate ambient clue (the player sees the saver long before the log).
- **S10 — Leslie's willful blindness.** She saw the cash and chose the
  question she didn't ask.
- **S11 — Jonathan's pattern.** He reads other people's private words and
  answers *as them* — Rebecca lived it (mail, letters) years before.
- **S12 — The 2:14 AM login.** Someone used Casey's PC the night she
  disappeared.

## Reveal moves (secret → wrong mouth → discovery)

The core mechanic: a secret is inert until it appears where it shouldn't.

1. **S9 in GhostBridge's mouth** — the recovered IM log shows the code word.
   Player + Sadie's logs know only two people had it. → *discovery:
   `stolen-intimacy` — whoever GhostBridge is, he had access to their private
   world.* (This is the slice's "bracelet" move, canonized.)
2. **S3 vs Chad's jealousy** — Chad's angry words in the parking lot ("some
   guy she talks to at 2am") prove he *didn't know who* — a guilty man
   wouldn't be jealous of himself. → exonerating clue hiding inside the
   incriminating scene.
3. **S6 vs S8** — Frank saw a *clean dark 4x4*; Chad's truck is lifted, loud,
   unmistakable in this town. One sighting exonerates both public suspects at
   once. → *discovery: `the-clean-truck`.*
4. **S11 in Rebecca's mouth** — her email/letter: "he used to answer my
   letters as me. Word for word like me. Check who's been answering you." →
   converts `stolen-intimacy` from *how* to *who-shaped*.
5. **S2 surfacing via Sam** — his forwarded board letter + "you asked me a
   question" → gives the player the *motive* spine (pipeline) that the ledger
   copy then proves.
6. **S12, the machine itself** — file modified-dates after Oct 10, the
   half-emptied Recycle Bin, the diary's last-modified 2:14 AM Oct 11. The
   only person with house access, PC access, and everything to lose. →
   *finale discovery: `the-house` — end of season.*
7. **S7's decoy arc** — Angel's changed statement makes Act 2 look like it's
   about her; her raw apology email resolves her into a witness, teaching the
   player that this game exonerates as often as it accuses.
