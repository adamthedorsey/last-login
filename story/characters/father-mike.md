# Father Mike

**One line:** The priest at St. Mark's, who heard something in confession he
cannot repeat and has been carrying it since.
**Function:** Texture and moral pressure. **No ledger row, by design.**

## Who they are
Runs St. Mark's, opens the fellowship hall at eight every morning for search
volunteers, holds a joint vigil and asks that "all denominations welcome" be
underlined twice. Weeks before Casey disappeared, Jonathan said something
oblique to him about money that isn't clean. He is bound by the seal. His
unease is readable; his words are not.

He exists to prove the season's thesis from a different angle: knowing
something and being unable to say it is its own kind of complicity, and the
town is full of people in that position.

## Voice
Warm, wry, unpretentious. Reaches the player only through a church web page
someone else maintains and a prayer chain.

> "A porch light left on." — `web.stmarks`, the church's own tagline, and a
> quiet rhyme with Casey's unfinished chorus in `file.songs`.

## What they know
Partial on S1, under seal. Partial on S6 — Frank hints at things in low
moments.

## On the machine
`web.stmarks`, `email.church.prayer`.

## Open
Everything. He is deliberately unresolved and should stay that way unless a
later season needs him.
