# Dr. Winfred "Win" Sparks

**One line:** The town doctor who writes the scripts, keeps Saturday hours
for cash patients from three counties, and is a top-decile prescriber in
Prescott's own paperwork.
**Function:** The brains and the cover-up. The name at the center of the
pipeline (S1) and the one Purvis tells the player not to say out loud.

## Who they are
Has practiced in Humble for decades and did not open on a Saturday for a heart
attack in ten years — until March, when he started opening every Saturday.
Prescott's Region 4 bulletin names him at 412 scripts in Q3, up 71 percent,
and instructs the rep to keep him close: speaker programs, honoraria, the trip
to Scottsdale.

He is not a cackling villain. He is a man who found out that the thing he was
already being praised for paid cash.

The initials in Casey's copied ledger page are "W.S." — "there is one W.S. in
this town and everybody calls him Win."

## Voice
Never speaks in shipped content. He exists as initials, a script count, a
waiting room full of out-of-county plates, and a line in a sales bulletin.
Keep him at that distance: he is scarier as a number.
