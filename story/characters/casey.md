# Casey Taylor

**One line:** Sixteen, a junior at Franklin Walker, the only person in Humble
County who noticed what the pills were doing and went looking for proof.
**Function:** The victim, and the author of almost everything the player
reads. The game is her machine.

## Who they are
Born 1981. Lives with her stepmother Leslie and Leslie's husband Jonathan in a
house they moved into in June. Her birth mother June died in the spring; her
father Frank is out on Route 9 in a trailer, drinking, still able to skip a
rock eleven times.

Curious, funny, a little lonely, and more competent with the machine than any
adult around her — but not an elite hacker (`canon.md`). She knows HTML, files
and directories, and enough about the early internet to get somewhere she
shouldn't with an adult walking her through it.

Her screen name is `SunflwrC81` — sunflower, June's flower, plus her birth
year. On IRC she is **junebug**. She keeps everything: the drawing Sadie made
her in second grade, a chain letter she saved purely as evidence against
Angel, stories she never finishes.

## Voice
All lowercase in IM and in her own files. Ends hard thoughts with a joke and
then doesn't quite land it. Writes to herself in brackets. Never melodramatic
on purpose, which is what makes the diary land.

> "i'm ok. theres just. stuff at the house. i'll tell u when i'm sure"
> — `im.sadie`

> "[not history notes. if you found this you were looking.]"
> — `file.ledger-copy`

When she is scared she gets shorter, not longer. Compare the Oct 8 log
(`file.ns-log-oct8`) where she answers a frightening line with "ok. you're
real. that's the part that scares me."

## What they know
S1 (grew from suspicion to certainty), S2, S3, S7, S9 fully. She suspects S14
without understanding it — "that file is ON MY COMPUTER. it's not ANYWHERE."
She never learns S4, S11, or S13. She never knows her stepfather has been
reading everything she writes.

## On the machine
Everything not written by someone else. The load-bearing pieces:
`trash.diary` (the smoking gun and her voice at its rawest), `file.ledger-copy`
(what she risked), `a.page` (what she got), `audio.voice-diary` ("it's not
mine to tell"), `file.songs`, `file.english-journal`, `file.riverstory`,
`file.lists`, `web.casey-page`, `file.datebook-1997`, `im.sadie`,
`file.ns-log-oct8`, `trash.bl-log`, `cd.oxytera`, `cd.todo`,
`email.sent.sadie`, `email.sent.mom`. Her login is `login.casey`.

## Open
Whether she ever suspected Jonathan specifically, or only "the house."
Nothing in her writing names him, which is deliberate — she was protecting
Leslie from a suspicion she couldn't prove.
