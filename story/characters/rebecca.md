# Rebecca Wright

**One line:** Jonathan's first wife, two towns over, who read about a missing
girl and recognized her ex-husband's name in the list of family.
**Function:** The Act 3 corroborator (S11) — and the season's misdirection
engine. Every true word she writes points the player at the wrong conclusion.

## Who they are
Married to Jonathan for six years, 1985–91. He read all of her mail, and
answered some of it as her — her cousin got letters in her voice for a year
and never suspected. When she left, he told her calmly that no one would
believe her, and for six years no one did.

She started her letter to a dead girl's mailbox four times before she sent it.
She is doing the bravest thing in the season and she is handing the player a
loaded gun pointed at the wrong target.

## Voice
Plain, controlled, short paragraphs, the control itself the tell. Repeats
herself when it matters. Signs with initials.

> "He pays attention, and then he becomes the person you trust."
> — `email.rebecca`

> "Ask him about my mail. 1989 to 1991. Ask him."
> — `email.rebecca`

## What they know
S11 fully. Partial on nothing else — she has no knowledge of the pipeline, the
meeting, or the night. She is reasoning from a pattern, and her reasoning is
correct about the man and wrong about the crime.

## On the machine
`email.rebecca` (grants `who-shaped`), `im.sadie` prompt `vigil` (she
approached Sadie at St. Mark's: "was she writing to someone who sounded
exactly right?").
