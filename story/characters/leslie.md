# Leslie Wright

**One line:** The woman Casey calls Mom — her stepmother, who married
Jonathan in June and has no idea what is on that computer.
**Function:** Texture and tragedy. **No ledger row, by design** — not
everyone who knows something is a puzzle piece.

## Who they are
Frank's second wife; when that marriage ended in 1996 Casey stayed with her,
because she was the steady one. Married Jonathan Wright June 14, 1997.
Works days. Feeds the family, worries about the phone bill, keeps June's
sunflowers going in her own yard for a girl who lost her mother in the spring.

She saw the new money and chose the question she didn't ask (S10). She is not
a villain and not an obstacle. She is an ordinary, caring guardian who is
going to find out, after the season ends, what she was married to.

## Voice
Affectionate, practical, slightly harried. Capitalizes properly. Uses dashes
and "I mean it." Says love you at the end of everything.

> "there's leftover casserole in the fridge, the GOOD tupperware not the
> mystery one in the back. Feed Buster. Homework before computer, I mean it"
> — `email.mom.dinner`

Her warmth has to stay completely uncomplicated. It is devastating on the
second read precisely because she is not hinting at anything.

## What they know
S10 fully. Partial on S1 (she has seen the money) and S11 (she is uneasy and
does not know why). Nothing else. She stays willfully blind through the
finale; her awakening is Season 2 fuel.

## On the machine
`email.mom.dinner`, `email.sent.mom`. Mentioned in `email.church.prayer`
("hold Leslie, Jonathan, and Frank") and `im.sadie`. That is deliberately all.

## Open
Nothing needed for Season 1. Her Season 2 arc is the whole open question.
