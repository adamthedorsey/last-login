# nightshift

**One line:** The adult on IRC who taught Casey how to get inside Prescott,
and who set the meeting she never came back from.
**Function:** The season's apparent villain and actual ally. The engine of
the double reading (`who-knows-what.md`).

## Who they are
An adult. Says he has been building a case against Sparks for two years and
knew about him before Casey did. Technically capable: PGP, a remote console
he opened during the Prescott job and never closed, the discipline to
tell her to delete her logs.

He was frightened for her and he handled her badly. He fed her fear to keep
her close ("people who do what i do have ways of knowing things"), pushed her
toward a night meeting, and told her to come alone and tell nobody — which
removed every person who might have kept her alive. That he meant well does
not make it not his fault. The reveal must **re-read** his behavior, not
excuse it.

## Voice
Lowercase, unhurried, never uses two words where one will do. Declarative.
Calls her "casey" at the start of a sentence when he wants her to settle.
Signs off with "goodnight" — the same word the remote console types.

> "i know a lot of things, casey. that's the job."
> — `trash.bl-log`

> "good girl. it will all be over after tonight."
> — `trash.bl-log`. Read once, it is a handler soothing an asset. Read twice,
> it is a man who thinks he is about to end this. Every one of his lines has
> to work both ways.

## What they know
S1, S2, S3 (he set it), S4, S9 (as her handle, and via the console as the
pact word), S14. He does **not** know S5 — what happened at the bend. Nobody
the player can reach does.

## On the machine
`file.ns-log-oct8` (the Oct 8 proof-of-self), `trash.bl-log` (the Oct 10
arrangement), `remote.nightshift-checkin` (the console, watched live),
`evt.nightshift-on` and the `nightshift` conversation (the sting), the
`the-watcher` discovery, and `a.page` — the file he walked her into taking.
