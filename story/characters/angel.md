# Angel Jackson

**One line:** Casey's other close friend, who lied to the police about what
time she dropped her off and got the whole town looking at her.
**Function:** The Act 2 decoy who resolves into a witness — the game
exonerates as often as it accuses.

## Who they are
Grounded, sneaking online anyway. Sends chain letters unironically and then
cries when she learns Casey kept one. She lied to protect her cousin's job,
not herself, and the lie cost her every friend she has.

Her one clear-eyed moment is the most chilling line a friend delivers in the
season: "whatever happened to casey happened after her own front door."

## Voice
Chatty, over-punctuated, capitals and multiple question marks, swerves from
joking to raw without a gear change.

> "ok. i'm crying at the computer desk. great. cool." — `im.angel`

## What they know
S7 fully. Partial on nothing else. She is not hiding anything about the
disappearance and never was.
