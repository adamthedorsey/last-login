# Frank Taylor

**One line:** Casey's father, drinking alone in a trailer off Route 9, who
saw the vehicle at the bend and will not tell the sheriff.
**Function:** Public suspect number two, and the eyewitness who exonerates
both public suspects at once (S6).

## Who they are
Divorced from June, then married and divorced from Leslie; his drinking ended
the second marriage in 1996 and he moved out to the wooded land off Route 9.
Has "the old trouble" on his record. Believes, correctly, that a drunk with a
record who lives on the road where the girl vanished will not be believed.

He taught Casey to skip rocks at the bend. He can still do eleven. Her record
is six. He can name every engine in the county with his eyes shut, and that
one useless skill is the season's most important piece of testimony.

He gave his daughter the name junebug, after her mother.

## Voice
Never writes. Reaches the player only through his sister quoting him, which
keeps him at exactly the right distance.

> "It wasn't the Daniels boy. That truck of his you can hear from the church.
> This one was somebody who washes his car."
> — Frank, via `email.ruth.yourdad`

## What they know
S6 fully. Partial on S5 — he saw lights stop, not what happened. Nothing
about the pipeline. He does not know his daughter was investigating anything.

## Open
Whether he ever tells anyone official. Whether he saw more than he said even
to Ruth.
