# Prescott Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

**One line:** The Stamford, Connecticut maker of Oxytera CR — the fictional
analogue for Purdue Pharma, and the reason Humble is coming apart.
**Function:** The other end of the pipe. Casey's original target, the source
of the material on the floppy, and the season's argument that the local crime
is a local symptom.

## What they are
Founded 1952 by Dr. Raymond E. Prescott, making antiseptics and earwax
remedies. Now selling a controlled-release opioid into Appalachia faster than
anywhere else in the country, with Region 4 ranked number one for four
consecutive quarters.

**Not a cartoon.** No document says "we know we're killing people." The truth
is assembled from ordinary corporate paper read together: a bulletin coaching
reps on what "persistent" pain means, a top-prescriber list with a reward
attached, a memo instructing field staff not to volunteer county overdose
numbers, a spreadsheet by county. The company's own line is that there is no
Humble problem, only an undertreatment problem that happens to be visible in
Humble.

## Voice
Two registers, and the gap between them is the whole indictment.

Public (`web.prescott*`): serene, philanthropic, "Committed to a Pain-Free
Tomorrow," pain as the fifth vital sign, dependence "reported to be very
small."

Internal (`a.page`, `cd.q3`): brisk, numerate, entirely amoral without ever
being villainous. "Reward it. Dr. Sparks is a believer."

## On the machine
`web.prescott`, `web.prescott-about`, `web.prescott-products`,
`web.prescott-board`, `web.prescott-careers` — the public face, restored
October 14 after a month of "maintenance" (that outage is the readable
tell that they audited the intrusion). `a.page` (the raw dump, PGP-locked),
`cd.q3` (the territory summary on the disc), `cd.oxytera` (what Casey could
prove from the library alone), `browserHistory` (someone visiting their board
page at 2:19 AM on the 11th).

## Open
Whether Prescott ever traced the intrusion to Casey. Whether anyone from the
company ever spoke to anyone in Humble. **Do not connect them directly** —
"evil pharma sends assassin after teenage hacker" is the exact story
`canon.md` forbids.
