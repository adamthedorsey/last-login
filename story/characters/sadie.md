# Sadie Thompson

**One line:** Casey's best friend since second grade, seventeen, keeper of
the safe word, and the only other person in this county actually looking.
**Function:** The emotional spine, and the player's **parallel investigator**
— path B for three discoveries.

## Who they are
Draws. Reads every true-crime book the library has, which is why she is the
one who thinks to bike out to Frank's trailer. Fiercely loyal and openly
angry that nobody is doing anything. Knows she is not taken seriously — "even
if nobody listens to a fat girl with a sketchbook. watch me."

She minted the safe word on Oct 3 and she will not tell the player what it is,
which is exactly right: she is protecting a friend she still believes is
coming back.

## Voice
Lowercase, fast, run-on when upset, capitalizes single words for force. Asks
questions she then answers herself.

> "she tells me everything. since second grade. and she wouldn't tell me
> this. that's the part that keeps me up." — `im.sadie` prompt `intro`

> "i'm not telling you it. it's ours." — `im.sadie` prompt `about-her`

## What they know
S9 fully. By the playable present she has also acquired S6 (Frank told her
Wednesday) and partial S11 (Rebecca found her at Friday's vigil) — that week
of her own investigating is what lets her carry second-path reveals. Partial
on S2, S3, S7. Nothing about the pipeline.

## On the machine
`im.sadie` — both the Oct 3 log and the whole live conversation, whose
prompts `intro`, `frank` and `vigil` grant `the-meeting`, `the-clean-truck`
and `who-shaped`. `email.sadie.please`, `email.sadie.notchad`,
`email.sent.sadie`, `web.sadie-page`, `photo.lockers`, `evt.sadie-knock` and
`evt.sadie-idle` (she goes quiet late; nothing happened, she is just sixteen
and it is late).
