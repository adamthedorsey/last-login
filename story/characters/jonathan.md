# Jonathan "Jon" Wright

**One line:** Casey's stepfather of four months, manager of the Value-Med
chain, and the man who read every private word she ever typed.
**Function:** The hands of the crime. And the answer the player is
supposed to reach about nightshift — wrongly.

## Who they are
Married Leslie June 14, 1997. Manages Value-Med, which has grown from one
Main St. store to three county locations while Sparks' Saturday hours filled
them. He keeps the ledger and skims the cash.

His real talent is attention. Rebecca's letter names it exactly: "He pays
attention, and then he becomes the person you trust." He read his first
wife's mail for years and answered her cousin *as her*. He has had Casey's
password since the day he set her machine up in the new house, and he told
her so as a joke.

He is not a monster in the room. He is pleasant, useful, hooks up the
computer, tells her to change her password. That is what makes the finale
land: the player has been reading his note on her desktop since minute one.

## Voice
Warm, brief, faintly instructional. Signs "— J". The only thing he writes
that the player ever sees is nine lines long and it is the whole character.

> "new house, same rules: don't install junk off the web... ps. change your
> password. i guessed it in one try. ONE."
> — `file.readme-first`

If he ever speaks again in content, he must never sound sinister. He sounds
like a stepdad who is trying.

## What they know
S1, S2, S3 (he read it), S5, S9 (the screen saver, and the logs), S10, S11,
S12, S13. He does **not** know S4 — he has no idea who nightshift actually
is, and never spoke to him. He assumes, like the player will, that some man
online was working Casey. He was right, and it did not matter.

## On the machine
`file.readme-first` (the note), `trash.diary` and `file.modem-log` (his
traces, 2:14 AM and the nightly sessions), `computer.bootWarning` (2:31 AM,
the power cut), `email.mom.dinner` and `email.sent.mom` (mentioned, warmly,
as "your husband"), `email.rebecca` (the pattern, in her words),
`web.valuemed` (his stores, donating flashlights to the search).

## Open
What happened at the bend (— permanently, for Season 1). Whether Leslie
ever suspects. What he thought he was going to do when he drove out there.
