# June Taylor

**One line:** Casey's birth mother, dead of an Oxytera CR overdose in the
spring of 1997.
**Function:** The wound the whole season grows out of. She never appears.

## Who they are
Prescribed Oxytera CR for pain, became dependent, died of an overdose. Married
to Frank, then not. The town called it an individual failure, the way it
called all of them individual failures — this is the year before anyone had
the word "epidemic" for it (`canon.md`).

She grew sunflowers. Frank called their daughter **junebug** — little June.

## Voice
None. She is never quoted, never writes, never appears in a photograph the
player can open. That absence is the point: the player assembles her out of a
flower, a nickname, and a login hint.

## What they know
Nothing. She died before the pipeline was visible.

## On the machine
`photo.sunflowers` (the caption says "mom's" and means Leslie's yard —
Leslie keeps June's flowers going, ). The login hint "moms flower + year"
(`computer.loginHint`) means June, and the year is 1997 — the machine's own
clock is the second half of the answer. `computer.saverText` is her name for
Casey, drifting across the idle screen all night.

`web.ardic-bulletin` is the closest the game comes to her death on the record: a September
law-enforcement bulletin, posted on the agency's own public web server, listing the district's overdose deaths,
one line of which is a 38-year-old woman in Humble County in March, prescribed,
single-drug. It does not say her name. Casey's reaction in `cd.oxytera` is the only place
she ever writes about her mother directly.

**How the player reaches it.** Casey's own bookmark `bm.ardic`, named only
`9714`, or a SearchHound query on any of oxytera, overdose, bulletin, abuse,
diversion, or drug intelligence. The bookmark is the guaranteed route; nothing
gates the page.

## Open
The exact date and circumstances of her death. Who prescribed for her —
**do not decide this yet.** If it was Sparks, the season becomes a revenge
story instead of a discovery story, and Casey stops being a witness.
